Command Reference Cheatsheet

All commands operate via In-Game Team Chat and Matrix #team-chat.

Raiding Durability
!c4 <target> - C4 required
!rocket <target> - Rockets required
!satchel <target> - Satchels required
!explo <target> - Explosive ammo required
!durability <target> - Full raid breakdown
Craft & Recycle
!craft [qty] <item> - Resource cost
!mix <item> - Mixing table recipes
!recycle [qty] <item> - Recycler output
!recbox <container> - Recycle box contents
Monuments & Puzzles
!monument <name> - Monument overview
!rad <monument> - Radiation protection
!keycard [color] - Puzzle requirements
Turrets & Defense
!turret-add <name> - Record turret coords
!turret-check [name] - 40m interference check
!sam-delay [seconds] - Auto re-arm delay
!sam-voice - 10s voice alert countdown
Automation Triggers
!auto-on <time> <switch> - Delayed on
!auto-off <time> <switch> - Delayed off
!day-on / !night-on - Solar cycle trigger
!teamoffline-on <switch> - Sleep defense
!alarms / !activated - Alarm history log
Team Telemetry
!proximity / !nearby - Teammate distances
!afk / !afk-all - Inactive team standstills
!death [player] - Recent death coordinates
!lead [player] - Team leader pin tracking
!kick <player> - Remove teammate
Storage & Upkeep
!contents <box> - Inspect container slots
!contains <item> - Search all storage boxes
!upkeep - TC remaining protection time
!monitor <box> - Real-time item diff alerts
AI & Team Notes
!ai <question> - Ask AI (if enabled)
!note <name> <text> - Save team note
!notes - View stored team notes
!notes-clear - Wipe saved notes
Tactical Utilities
!timer <time> [msg] - In-game reminder
!stopwatch [start|stop] - Elapsed timer
!calc <expression> - Quick calculator
!roll [max] / !fortune - Random rolls
!voice <text> - Speak in Matrix voice call
